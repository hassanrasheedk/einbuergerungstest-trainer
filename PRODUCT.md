# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Stack

SwiftUI, Swift Package built and installed with xtool (no Xcode project). iOS 17+.

## Users

One person preparing for the Einbürgerungstest in Nordrhein-Westfalen, with a fixed test date a few weeks away. Studies in short sessions on their own iPhone over the final days before the test.

## Product Purpose

Know all 310 questions that can appear in the test (300 general + 10 NRW) well enough to pass comfortably (17 of 33 needed). Success: every question answered correctly at least twice before test day, and mock exams passing reliably.

## Positioning

Built from the official BAMF Gesamtfragenkatalog (Stand 07.05.2025) with its exact wording, numbering and pictures, scoped to NRW, and paced by a plan that counts down to the real test date.

## Operating Context

Short phone sessions (5–20 min), often one-handed. The real test is on paper/PC, German only, one correct answer out of four, 33 questions in 60 minutes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Daily plan: spreads unseen questions over the days left (finishing two days early), then resurfaces mistakes with simple spaced repetition.
- Practice by topic, by catalog order, mistakes only, bookmarked.
- Mock exam: 30 general + 3 NRW, 60-minute timer, pass at 17.
- Full searchable catalog with the correct answer.
- German is primary; English translation and an English explanation are tap-to-reveal.
- All data stays on device. No accounts, no network.
- Answer key comes from an MIT-licensed community dataset, matched by text to the official PDF and reviewed; the official PDF itself contains no answers.

## Evidence on Hand

- `Sources/EinbuergerungTrainer/Resources/catalog.json` and `img_*.png`, generated by `tools/build_catalog.py` from the BAMF PDF.

## Product Principles

1. Exam fidelity: official wording, order and pictures; nothing paraphrased in German.
2. The next step is always obvious: the Today tab says what to do now.
3. Mistakes come back until they stick.
4. Calm and fast: no gamification noise, no waiting on animations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Dynamic Type, Dark Mode, VoiceOver labels on answer state, Reduce Motion honored.
